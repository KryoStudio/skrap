- Say that your project is "based on Skrap" or "a fork of Skrap".
- Use the name and logo to talk about Skrap itself: articles, videos, reviews, tutorials, links to the official download.
- Package the unmodified official release for a software store or package manager, under the Skrap name.

- Distribute a modified version under the name "Skrap", or with its logo.

## If you fork

Pick your own name and logo, replace the files in `assets/brand` and `app/src-tauri/icons`, generate your own update signing key, and keep the "Based on Skrap by Kryo Studio" notice required by the [NOTICE](NOTICE) file.
